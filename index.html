A zip code shouldn't decide who gets to be a scientist.

Across California, one in three high schools offers no AP science track. Rural counties average 1.4 hours from the nearest STEM outreach program.

We don't think that's a pipeline problem. It's an access problem — and access is something you can engineer.

  • 01

    Meet students where they are

    CSI shows up in many places — bringing resources and tools wherever students are.

  • 02

    Make curriculum portable

    What we build is designed to travel. No special setup, no prerequisites — just join and have fun.

  • 03

    Lead with high quality

    We don't trade rigor for reach. Strong content is the foundation, not the finishing touch.

  • 04

    Never charge a student

    Free for students, always. Supported by grants and a growing network of partners across industries.
